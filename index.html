Pre-start check app questions

What is a pre-start check?

A pre-start check is a walk-around and function check an operator does on a machine before starting work, to spot faults and hazards. DiggerSafe gives you a structured digital version you complete on your phone.

Does DiggerSafe replace my legal obligations?

No. DiggerSafe is a record-keeping tool. Always follow the manufacturer's manual, your site rules and the workplace health and safety laws that apply to you.

What machines can I add?

You can add any plant machine to your fleet, such as excavators, loaders, skid steers and tippers, then print a QR sticker for each one.

Do my operators need to install the app?

No. Operators scan the QR sticker on the machine and complete the check in their phone's browser, with no app install and no login.

Does DiggerSafe work offline?

Records save on your phone and sync to the cloud when you are back online.

How much does DiggerSafe cost?

There is a 14-day free trial. After that, Starter is $7.99 per month for up to 2 machines, Basic is $19.99 per month for up to 10 machines and Pro is $47.99 per month for up to 50 machines. Yearly plans are also available.

Is there an Android app?

Yes. DiggerSafe is available on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
