Plant hire pre-hire inspection checklist

If you hire out plant, a clear record of the machine's condition when it leaves and when it comes back protects you and your customer. Use this checklist at pick-up and again at return.

This is a general guide only. Always follow the manufacturer's operator manual, your site rules and the workplace health and safety laws that apply to you.

1. Record at pick-up

  • Machine ID, make, model and serial number
  • Hour meter reading and fuel level
  • Date, time and location of pick-up
  • Photos of all sides of the machine, the cab and any existing damage
  • Tyres or tracks, bucket or attachment, and glass condition
  • Attachments and accessories supplied (buckets, couplers, keys)
  • Safety items present: fire extinguisher, first aid kit, beacon, seat belt
  • Fluid levels checked and no obvious leaks
  • Customer name, operator name and signature

2. Brief the operator

  • Show where the controls, isolator and emergency stop are
  • Explain the pre-start check the operator must do each day
  • Point out known limits, such as ground conditions or attachment ratings
  • Provide your contact number for faults or breakdowns

3. Record at return

  • Hour meter reading and fuel level
  • Date, time and location of return
  • Photos of all sides of the machine and any new damage
  • Compare tyres or tracks, bucket or attachment and glass with the pick-up record
  • All attachments and accessories returned
  • Customer or operator signature

Frequently asked questions

Why record a machine's condition before and after hire?

A dated record with photos, hour meter and fuel level at pick-up and return gives both sides a clear reference if there is a question about damage or usage.

Can DiggerSafe record photos and signatures?

Yes. DiggerSafe lets you attach photos and a signature to an inspection, records the time and location, and creates a PDF report you can share with the customer.

Do hire customers need the app?

Operators can scan the QR sticker on the machine and complete a check in their phone's browser, with no app install and no login.
