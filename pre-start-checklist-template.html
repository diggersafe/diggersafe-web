Free printable pre-start checklist template

A general plant pre-start checklist you can print and use for excavators, loaders, skid steers and similar machines. Tick each item, note any defects, and sign at the bottom.

This is a general guide only. Always follow the manufacturer's operator manual, your site rules and the workplace health and safety laws that apply to you.

1. Walk-around (engine off)

  • Logbook and plates: serial number matches, decals legible, previous defects repaired
  • Engine oil, hydraulic oil and coolant levels
  • Fuel level; no fuel, oil or hydraulic leaks
  • Hoses and fittings: no chafing, cracks or weeping
  • Tyres or tracks: condition, pressure or tension, wheel nuts or track bolts
  • Boom, arm, lift arms and bucket or attachment: cracks, pins, bushes and cutting edge
  • Quick hitch or coupler locked and pinned
  • Guarding, steps, handrails and covers secure
  • Battery and isolator secure; grease points greased

2. Cab and controls

  • Seat belt fitted and working
  • Mirrors, camera and windows clean and undamaged
  • Controls move freely and return to neutral
  • Horn, lights, beacon and reversing or travel alarm working
  • Gauges and warning lights normal after start-up
  • Emergency stop or isolator within reach
  • Fire extinguisher and first aid kit present and in date

3. Operating test

  • Warm up, then operate all functions through full range
  • Steering, travel, and brakes (service, swing and parking) work correctly
  • No unusual noises, smells or vibration
  • Ground conditions and overhead and underground services checked before work
