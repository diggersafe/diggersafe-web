Tipper truck and trailer pre-start checklist

Use this tipper truck and trailer pre-start checklist before each shift. It covers the walk-around, the coupling and tipping body, the cab, and a brake and tip test.

This is a general guide only. Always follow the manufacturer's operator manual, your site rules and the workplace health and safety laws that apply to you.

1. Walk-around (engine off)

  • Tyres: pressure, cuts, damage and wheel nuts, including on trailers
  • Lights, indicators, brake lights and reflectors working and clean
  • Engine oil, coolant and hydraulic oil levels; fuel level
  • No fuel, oil or hydraulic leaks under the truck or trailer
  • Tipping body, tailgate latches and hinges in good condition
  • Tipping ram and hoist: no leaks or damage; body prop or safety stay available
  • Coupling: pins, safety chains and breakaway system secure
  • Brake and electrical lines connected and not chafed
  • Load restraint and tarp equipment in good order
  • Mirrors secure; registration and plates visible

2. Cab

  • Seat belt fitted and working
  • Windscreen, wipers and mirrors clean and undamaged
  • Horn, reversing alarm and camera working
  • Gauges and warning lights show normal after start-up
  • Fire extinguisher and first aid kit present and in date

3. Operating test

  • Service brake and parking brake hold
  • Steering moves freely with no pull to one side
  • Raise and lower the tipping body with the area clear, and check for smooth movement
  • Before tipping: check ground conditions, level, and the location of overhead services

Frequently asked questions

What should a tipper truck pre-start check include?

It should include tyres, lights, fluid levels, leaks, the tipping body, tailgate and ram, couplings on any trailer, the cab, and a test of the brakes and tipping function.

Why check overhead services before tipping?

Raising a tipping body near powerlines or other overhead services is a serious hazard. Check the area is clear before you raise the body, and follow your site rules.
